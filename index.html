How it works

The federal credit for increasing research activities (section 41 of the Internal Revenue Code) rewards companies that develop new or improved products, software or processes through experimentation. It is claimed on Form 6765 with the income tax return, and the IRS expects documentation that ties each dollar of wages and expenses to qualified research.

That documentation is hardest to build at year end. The service collects it during the year: payroll comes in, each engineer confirms monthly the share of time spent on each project, and supplies, computer use and contract research are tagged by project. At year end you get the calculation of qualified research expenses, the draft Form 6765 and the file of supporting records.

A qualified small business can apply part of the credit against payroll taxes; the service prepares the election and the quarterly figures for Form 8974. Your CPA reviews everything and files the return.

Questions

Who can claim the R&D credit?

Businesses that develop new or improved products, software or processes through experimentation, as described in section 41 of the Internal Revenue Code.

Is this tax advice?

No. We prepare the calculation and documentation from your data; your CPA reviews and files the return. We are not a CPA firm and do not represent you before the IRS.
